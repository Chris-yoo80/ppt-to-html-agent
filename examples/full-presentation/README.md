# Complete presentation example

This fictional example shows that the agent can build a presentation from beginning to end. Its seven scenes cover contents, overview, three main-discussion scenes, timeline and conclusion.

The structure draws on the rhythm of a full working presentation: orient the audience, establish the scope, explain the evidence, propose the work, show the schedule and close with a decision. It does not reuse company names, figures, wording or internal material.

All figures are newly written fictional data. Reach is 1,500 people, clicks are 300 events and purchases are 45 events. Therefore purchases/clicks is 15.0% and purchases/reach is 3.0%. Event counts and people counts remain explicitly labeled because their units differ.

Build both versions:

```sh
python tools/build_full_presentation.py
```

Outputs: `docs/full-presentation.html` and `docs/full-presentation.en.html`.
