# Three independently authored fictional motion studies

These examples were created for this public project. They contain no client
slides, operational records, maps, warehouse drawings, videos, or campaign data.
They are hand-authored motion presets, not proof of automatic PPT conversion.

## Delivery network

Twelve fictional delivery stops and a depot are placed on an invented coordinate
plane. Compare two prescribed route plans, each serving every stop once with
three vehicles. Show orders, regions, new paths and vehicle motion in sequence.
The displayed reduction compares Euclidean segment lengths on this fictional
plane. It is not a road-network result or an optimizer's performance claim.

## Marketing journey

Use fictional counts of 1,200 reached, 360 engaged, 72 converted and 24 returning
people to explain a campaign journey. The 6% figure is 72 divided by 1,200. Dots
are symbolic and do not represent an actual counted population or attribution
model. The return path is a conceptual retention loop.

## Warehouse operations

Draw an invented isometric warehouse with receiving, racks, picking lanes and
shipping. Animate boxes and a picking vehicle along prescribed paths. This is a
process illustration; no physical facility or throughput result is represented.

Each story lasts 24 seconds, supports four selectable explanation phases, and
ends without looping. Pause, replay and scrub controls are included. Reduced
motion disables automatic playback and initially shows the final overview.
